ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave each year.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to 10 days into the next calendar year.

REMOTE WORK POLICY

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

HEALTH INSURANCE

Dental coverage begins after 90 days of employment.

PARENTAL LEAVE

Employees are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave after one year of employment.
